# Management workspaces
The staff link on the account page opens the user's assigned workspace. Leadership and office staff use individual Supabase accounts with a verified TOTP factor. The first leadership assignments belong in the database owner’s provisioning process. Required fields are the existing auth user ID, role and office in management_members. Keep at least one separate legacy administrator for legacy-only operations. A CEO can assign an existing registered account to an employee office; this cannot replace a leadership role.

Founder sees strategy, recorded homestay booking value and commission, and approves expense, finance and critical requests. Amounts are booking records. CEO assigns tasks and email follow-ups, reviews partner changes and product release requests, and manages employee offices. Compose Email opens the device mail client. Completing the task records the operator’s confirmation. Co-founder edits the existing bilingual CMS and submits pending partner applications for review. CEO approval activates the actual partner record. A PWA release decision records an internal approval; deployment is a separate action. Office staff record attendance and reports once per India calendar day, register tourist requests, link existing booking codes, and submit expenses. Existing booking creation, inventory, payouts, and settlement continue in their original modules.
